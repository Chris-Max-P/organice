# 11: Checklist

**What to build:** a task carries a checklist for shopping, inventory and packing lists, sub-tasks and needs ("need a van"). An item is text plus a checkbox. As soon as an item needs its own person, deadline or history, it becomes a task of its own — there are no nested task trees.

Data: adds `checklist_items` (tasks design, "Data model").
