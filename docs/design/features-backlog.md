# Features backlog

- **Data export** — the core team can take its data out of the app and use it
  elsewhere in raw form. optional: full export of all at once
- **Document meetings**
- **Inventory management** — items can be linked to tasks or to areas on the
  festival site.
- **Decision making**
- **Finances** — the full picture beyond the dashboard's key figures
  ([admin-dashboard.md](./admin-dashboard.md)): income and spending in detail,
  including the budget used by tasks.
- **Guest overview**
- **Car pooling**
- **Timetable** — food, workshops, music.
- Multi-user, multi-event support
- area-dashboard with blocks for each area that collect the information for that area (basically a filter)
